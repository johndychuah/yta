# Photography placement

Photographs must illustrate the specific action or subject beside them. A pleasant generic meeting is not a substitute for tax compliance, IPO preparation, cash-flow recovery or market entry.

Use each photo at one editorial placement across the site. Brand and professional-body logos are intentionally shared. English and Chinese versions of the same China–Malaysia Desk placement share their photos for translation consistency.
